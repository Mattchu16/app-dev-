# My Favorite Series of All Time
## Breaking Bad (2008)
Main Cast: Bryan Cranston, Aaron Paul, Anna Gunn, Dean Norris.

## Synopsis:
Breaking Bad follows Walter White, a high‑school chemistry teacher diagnosed with stage‑three lung cancer, who partners with former student Jesse Pinkman to manufacture and sell methamphetamine in order to secure his family’s financial future

## My thoughts/Series Experience: 
The series perfectly displayed the cruel and life-destructing effect of drug abuse in different ways. Not only it nailed the realism aspect, but it also did a conveyed a compelling drama that had me in the edge of my seat. I found myself binge-watching the episodes, it was that hooking. Bryan Cranston and Aaron Paul, the actors who played Walter and Jesse, did a fantastic job in portraying their characters.
